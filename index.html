Options Backtester Overview

Test complex options strategies against years of historical data. Whether you trade simple calls and puts or multi-leg spreads, the Options Backtester lets you validate your strategy before risking real capital. Configure entry and exit conditions using technical indicators, set strike selection rules, and analyze detailed P/L outcomes.

Features

22+ Strategy Types

Spreads, straddles, strangles, iron condors, calendars, diagonals, and more — all configurable.

Custom Entry/Exit Rules

Define conditions using moving averages, RSI, MACD, price thresholds, and more.

Greeks Analysis

See Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega across your positions over time.

Historical Options Data

Real options chain data via Polygon.io for accurate strike pricing and premium calculations.

Results & Analysis

After each backtest, review detailed trade logs with entry/exit prices, P/L per trade, equity curves, drawdown analysis, and key metrics like Win Rate, Profit Factor, and Sharpe Ratio. Identify which market conditions favor your strategy and refine your approach.
